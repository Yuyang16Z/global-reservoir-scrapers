# with '#' are comments. Options follow the URL, separated by spaces (write \s
# for a space inside a pattern):

#   grep=REGEX     print matches of REGEX in the response text

#   https://www.koski.gov.tr/koski/bagbasi-baraj-degerleri grep=\d\d/11/2026.{0,200}
